**1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**

* Unwanted touching or physical contact.
* Inappropriate comments or sexual jokes.
* Sending unwanted messages or pictures.
* Repeatedly asking someone out after they say no.
* Any behaviour that makes someone feel uncomfortable or unsafe

**2. What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?**

* Speak up if it is safe.
* Move away from the situation if needed.
* Keep a record of the incident and any evidence.
* Report it to HR or the Internal Committee.
* Support the affected person and encourage them to report it.
