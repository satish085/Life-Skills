**1. Paraphrase the Grit video in 1–2 lines.**

Grit means having passion and patience to work towards a goal. Success comes from continuous effort, not just talent.

**2. Paraphrase the Growth Mindset video in 1–2 lines.**

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills and intelligence through learning, practice and effort.

**3. What is Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and decisions can shape our future. We should focus on things that are in our control.

**4. Key points to build a Growth Mindset:**

* Take responsibility for my learning.
* Stay confident and positive.
* Understand concepts properly.
* Never give up on difficult problems.
* Learn from mistakes.
* Put in consistent effort.
* Stay open to new challenges.

**5. What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?**

* Take responsibility for my learning.
* Break big tasks into smaller tasks.
* Keep trying until I solve a problem.
* Learn different ways to solve problems.
* Accept feedback and improve.
* Learn from my mistakes.
* Focus on improvement rather than results.
