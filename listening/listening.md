**1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?**

* Give full attention to the speaker.
* Avoid distractions.
* Don't interrupt while someone is speaking.
* Maintain eye contact.
* Show interest through body language.
* Ask questions if something is unclear.
* Take notes when needed.
* Repeat main points to confirm understanding.

**2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?**

* Listen carefully without interrupting
* Repeat important points in your own words.
* Understand the speaker feelings.
* Ask questions to clear doubts
* Confirm that you understood message correctly.

**3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?**

* Getting distracted by my thoughts.
* Background noise.
* Feeling sleepy or tired.
* Losing focus when I am angry or upset


**4. What can you do to improve your listening?**

* Give full attention to the speaker.
* Avoid distractions.
* Don't interrupt others
* Ask questions when needed.
* Take notes during important discussions.


**5. When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?**

When I am in group discussions, I sometimes hesitate to share my opinions.


**6. When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?**

When I get frustrated or angry, sometimes I speak loudly and react without thinking.


**7. When do you switch into Passive Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?**

When I feel upset with someone but don't want to express it directly, i sometimes stay silent.

**8. How can you make your communication assertive?**

* Express my thoughts clearly and confidently.
* Respect other people opinions
* Speak calm without getting angry.
* Listen to others before responding.
* Say no politely when needed.
* Avoid blaming others and focus on solutions
