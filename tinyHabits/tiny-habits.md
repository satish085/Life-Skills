**2. How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.**

* B = MAP is a simple formula to understand how habits are formed.
* M – Motivation: Having interest to do something.
* A – Ability: Making sure the task is easy to do.
* P – Prompt: Having a reminder to start the habit.
